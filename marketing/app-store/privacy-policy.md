# Machole — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-20_

Machole does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no servers; the app makes no network calls at runtime.

## Camera

Machole accesses your camera to display your face in the on-screen overlay and, if you choose, include it in screen recordings. The camera feed is processed entirely on your Mac. It is **never** transmitted off your device.

## Microphone

Machole accesses your microphone to capture audio for screen recordings and to drive the audio-reactive pulse on the overlay. Audio is processed entirely on your Mac. It is **never** transmitted off your device.

## Screen Recording

Machole accesses your screen, application windows, or selected areas to record them. Recordings exist only in the location you choose at save time. They are **never** transmitted off your Mac.

## Saved recordings

Files written by Machole live wherever you choose to save them. The app does not upload, copy, or share these files with any third party.

## On-device intelligence

The background blur, auto-frame, and close-up features use machine-learning models (Google's MediaPipe) that ship inside the app bundle. They run entirely on your Mac. No data is sent to Google, Apple, or any other party.

## Analytics, telemetry, crash reports

Machole collects **none** of these. The app does not include any analytics SDKs, telemetry pipelines, or crash reporting services.

## Accounts

Machole does not require any user account or sign-in. There is no user identifier of any kind.

## Children

Machole does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of Machole begins collecting any data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and you will be informed at first launch.
