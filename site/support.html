Support

Got a bug, a question, or a feature idea? Here's how to reach us.

Report a bug or request a feature

File an issue on GitHub: github.com/greenthread-ai/machole/issues. Please include your macOS version, Machole version, and steps to reproduce.

First-launch checklist

  • Grant Camera when prompted (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera).
  • Grant Microphone the same way.
  • Grant Screen Recording — Machole has to be restarted after enabling.

Email

For anything that doesn't fit a public issue, email hello@theitdept.au.
