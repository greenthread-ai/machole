Your face. In every recording.

macOS screen recorder with an always-on camera overlay. Screen, microphone, and system audio — recorded together, in one file, on your Mac.

Mac App Store Download .dmg

Apple Silicon & Intel · macOS Sonoma or later

🎥

Screen, window, or area

Pick a full display, one app window, or drag-select any rectangle. Microphone and system audio are mixed in automatically.

☀️

Always-on camera overlay

A circular face overlay floats above every app. Five gradient themes, three sizes, right-click for full control.

🧠

On-device ML

Background blur, auto-frame, and close-up framing — all running locally. Nothing leaves your Mac.

⏹️

Mid-recording controls

A persistent panel for pause, mute, and hiding your face. The panel itself is excluded from the recording.

🔒

Privacy-first

No accounts. No analytics. No telemetry. No network calls. Recordings save wherever you choose.

🎢

One file in

Screen video, microphone, and system audio are mixed into a single .mp4 (or .webm where .mp4 isn't supported).
